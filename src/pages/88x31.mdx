<h1 class="text-center">88x31</h1>

As you can see, I have a lot of 88x31 buttons in the footer. There are 2 sections for them:
- The first is for page information and some buttons that I like.
- The second is for friends' buttons, which link to their personal websites.

## Why I use them

88x31 buttons are a small piece of old web culture I really like. Before social media made
everything look the same, personal sites used tiny buttons like these to show off the things
you like and care about or the sites you wanted to share. No algorithms, no ads. Just people
linking to things they liked.

I keep using them because it still feels like a nice way to leave a trail of what I care
about, and to point visitors toward people or places worth checking out.

## Adding friend buttons

I only add buttons from people who are my friends.

If you want to add my button to your page, use the following snippet or something similar:

```html
<a href="https://louiszn.fyi" title="Cutie oreo" target="_blank">
    <img src="https://louiszn.fyi/assets/88x31/me.png" alt="Oreo cat" width="88" height="31"/>
</a>
```

Which will display the button like this:

<a href="/" title="Cutie oreo" target="_blank">
	<Image src="/assets/88x31/me.png" width={88} height={31} alt="Oreo cat" />
</a>

This way, I can update my button without needing you to update anything on your end!
But feel free to save it and host it locally if you prefer.
