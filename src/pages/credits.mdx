## Inspiration

The layout is heavily inspired by many websites on [Neocities](https://neocities.org/), especially [petrapixel's](https://petrapixel.neocities.org/).   I wrote everything from scratch without looking at the source, but the vibe is all theirs. Go check out their site, it's lovely! 💖

## Fonts

### [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/)

Used for headings and code. Its clean, blocky shapes give the site a techy, terminal-like feel and make section titles stand out.

### [Atkinson Hyperlegible](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/)

Used for body text. It was designed by the Braille Institute to make every character easy to tell apart, so the site stays comfortable to read for as many people as possible.
